AccountNote Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

The short version

  • Everything you enter is stored on your phone. There's no account, no sign-in and no server.
  • The developer doesn't collect, receive, sell or share any of your data.
  • If you pair with a partner, your entries go directly to your partner's phone over your own Wi-Fi or hotspot, encrypted. They never pass through the internet or any server.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

What's stored on your phone

Your transactions (amount, date, time, category, account, notes and tags), categories, accounts, budgets, recurring transactions and settings. They're kept in the app's private storage on your phone, where the developer can't see them.

If you pair with a partner, the app also keeps a read-only copy of what your partner shares with you, your partner's name, and a secret key used to encrypt your syncs.

If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your phone's backup. Google manages that backup under your account, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Partner sync

Partner sync is optional, and nothing is shared until you pair.

  • Pairing: one phone shows a QR code and the other scans it. The code holds the showing phone's ID, the name you chose to show, a one-time secret key and its address on your Wi-Fi. It works once, for 10 minutes.
  • What's shared: your transactions, categories, sub-categories, accounts and budgets. Entries you mark "Don't share" are never sent.
  • How: the two phones connect directly on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot. Everything sent is encrypted (AES-GCM) with the key from pairing, and each sync needs the other phone's owner to allow it. Nothing goes to the internet, the developer or anyone else.
  • Your partner's copy: your partner can see what you share but can't change it, and you can't change theirs.
  • Stopping: Settings › Partner › Remove deletes your partner's copy from your phone. To delete your entries from your partner's phone, ask them to remove you too.

Permissions

  • Camera: only to scan your partner's pairing QR code. No photos or video are saved or sent.
  • Local network / Wi-Fi: only to find your partner's phone on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot and sync with it.
  • Notifications: for budget alerts and recurring reminders, created on your phone. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or your phone's settings.

Files you export

Export creates a CSV file only when you ask for it, and you choose where it goes. Once you send or save it somewhere else, that app or service's own privacy policy applies. The file isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.
  • Reset all data in Settings deletes your own entries, budgets, recurring transactions, categories and accounts. If you're paired, your partner's copy of your entries is cleared at your next sync, and your partner's book stays on your phone until you remove your partner.
  • You can also delete any entry at any time.

Children

AccountNote is meant for adults. It isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect anyone's data.
