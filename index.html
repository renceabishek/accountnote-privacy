AccountNote Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

AccountNote is a personal expense tracker for Android. It works offline, and it has no accounts or sign-in. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

Information we collect

We don't collect any information. The developer has no server for this app and can't see your transactions, budgets, categories, accounts, tags or notes.

Where your data is stored

Everything you enter is stored in a database on your phone. If you uninstall the app or use Reset all data in Settings, it's deleted from the app.

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your phone's backup. That backup is managed by Google under your account, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Files you export

Export creates a CSV file only when you ask for it, and you choose where it goes. Once you send or save that file somewhere else, that app or service's own privacy policy applies. The file isn't encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.

Notifications

Budget alerts and recurring reminders are created on your phone. The app asks for permission to show them, and you can turn them off in the app's Settings or your phone's settings.

Ads, analytics and third parties

The app has no ads and no analytics or tracking tools, and it doesn't sell or share your data with anyone.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
